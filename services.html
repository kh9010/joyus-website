What we do, when you ask

AdviseDesignTellStrategizeChart

Five things we do — each one a wider question than the last.

01 — of 05

advise

You bring the problem. We help you see it clearly enough to decide.

Sometimes you already know the question and you just want a second pair of eyes. Sometimes you're weighing two options and need someone to argue both sides. Sometimes the recommendation is to do nothing. It always starts in the room, with the thinking.

  • Should we rebuild the brand, or just rename it?
  • Is the product landing with the audience we think we're talking to?
  • Do I have more options than I think I do?
  • Do I need a co-founder, or a hire?
02 — of 05

design

You don't have a solution yet. We design what it should be.

Design here isn't decoration — it's finding the shape of an answer that doesn't yet exist. The artifact is downstream. Sometimes that ends as a deck, sometimes a product, sometimes an identity. The real work is sitting with the question long enough to know what the solution wants to be.

  • What is this product, actually?
  • What should this brand stand for — and to whom?
  • We have the idea. What's the smallest real version of it?
  • Why do customers come back — and how do we make more of that?
03 — of 05

tell

The story that unifies the team is usually the same story that lands with investors and customers.

When a company struggles to explain itself, the story is rarely the problem — the telling is. We spend time with the people who know the business best and find the version of the story that lands the same way for investors, customers, new hires, and each other.

  • Why aren't investors seeing what we see?
  • How do we say what we do without sounding like everyone else?
  • How do we get our own team to believe the same thing?
  • How does our story earn trust?
04 — of 05

strategize

When the question is still open, we go deep with you until it isn't.

Sometimes you don't bring a defined problem — you bring a feeling that something isn't right, or a tangle you can't get to the bottom of. We become your strategic partner for as long as the question is open. The work is figuring out what to do.

  • What should we be doing in the next three months — and the next six?
  • What's working, what's not, and why?
  • Is it a retention problem, or a growth problem?
  • Is there an adjacent market we should be in?
05 — of 05

chart

Sometimes the question isn't what to do — it's where to go.

The market changed. Or the team did. Or the founder did. And the original plan stopped fitting. We work with you on the biggest version of the question — what this business is now, and where it goes next — before it becomes an emergency.

  • How do we make this a sustainable business?
  • Where will the growth in enterprise value come from?
  • Where did the market, the team, and the company stop agreeing?
  • What's my second act?
