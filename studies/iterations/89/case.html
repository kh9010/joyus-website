Joyus Case study

East West Club is a new members club in Seattle. Its value was big and vague: a new social club, not a country club. Vague is hard to join.

We worked out who it was for and what it meant to them at the same time, until the two fit.

Each time we pinned one, the other moved. So we kept moving both until they held still.
