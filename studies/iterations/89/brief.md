# Brief: one case study for Joyus, about East West Club. Say less.

You haven't seen earlier attempts unless your task says so. Everything you need is here plus the sources at the end.

## The one problem this page has

Earlier versions were accurate and detailed, and the detail buried the idea. A reader came away knowing facts about a club, not what the studio figured out. **This page is about one insight. Everything that does not carry it comes off.**

## The insight (Kahran, 8 Nov 2026)

East West Club (EWC) is a new members club in Seattle. Its value was big and vague: "a not-country-club, a new social club." To make it feel accessible and desirable to the right people, we had to work out what that value means to people, and that meant working out *who* those people are. Both were moving at once: who the club is for, and what it delivers. Fix one and the other shifts. **The work was finding where they hold still together: an equilibrium, not a guess at either side.** It worked: 88% of founding members signed, faster than the club expected.

A stranger who reads the page should be able to say back, in their own words: *"The club's value was vague. They worked out who it was for and what it meant to them at the same time, until the two fit."* If they can't, the page has failed, however good it looks.

## Who reads it

A founder or creative, sent the link by someone they trust, missing one piece, on their phone. The page should say "that's you, and you'd come out of this looking good."

## Subtraction rules

- **At most 3 phone screens** at 390px. Two is better.
- **At most about 120 words of our own text** on the whole page, not counting the club's quoted words and labels in the diagram.
- **One picture carries the idea:** a static, self-labelled diagram of the two moving things and where they meet. It must make sense with no tap and no caption below the fold.
- **One proof:** 88% of founding members signed, faster than expected. Say "of founding members". Never a raw count, never "before the club was built" or any timing we don't have.
- **Evidence is optional and small:** at most one piece of the club's own words or one piece of made work (a member card or the brand guide). The six member types may appear only as labels inside the diagram, never as a list of cards.
- **Cost and who:** one line: "Kahran and Divya, the two of us. What it cost: $5–10K."
- **Close:** "You don't need a brief. It starts small." and one small way in (an email, or "How would you have solved it?").
- **Two voices:** ours in one quiet style; the club's words upright with a short source. Little or no italic body text; it tires readers on a phone.
- **First screen must state, in plain sentences:** East West Club (in full), the vague value, and what we did. No metaphor before the facts.

## Hard rules (the repo is public)

- No names of the club's people, no prospect names. Kahran and Divya may be named.
- No money except $5–10K, no figure except 88%. No income or family details.
- Never "an easy weekday place".
- No visible placeholders, internal notes, § marks, empty boxes. The wordmark is the plain text "Joyus".
- Body text at least 16px, labels at least 13px, contrast at least 4.5:1. No em dashes in our words. noindex.
- Fine at desktop width too.
