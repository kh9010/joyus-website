Buttons

Buttons share one box. Reach for one and it takes room; the others give it.

Where you are

The place you are in holds the most room.

Choosing several

What you choose grows and takes its colour. What you leave stays, small.

A menu

One piece becomes many, then one again. Nothing appears from nowhere.

A list that opens

Arrangement is the pieces filling the frame. Open one and the rest make way without leaving.

A form

Arriving and leaving
