The bet, against the output

Who the customer is, and what has to be true for them to buy.

A founder or creative, sent by someone they trust, missing one piece.

Nearly everything we've made says: look at us. The bet needs it to say: that's you, and you'd come out of this looking good.

Three moves I'd start with

  • Name the situation firstThe hero and every Work line open on what's stuck, not on what we did. "For Klydo, we built..." becomes what Klydo was stuck on. Rows 1 and 2.
  • Make the client the one who finds itEach case study runs: came for this, it turned out to be that, and they did this with it. Your Packaging question already opens this way, and EWC's Tuesday already finds something. Neither does both yet. Rows 2, 4 and 5.
  • Replace fear with the hauntingThe July line, the projects that haunt you until they're made, is a cost of waiting that doesn't need dread. It goes on Say hi with a small first step. Rows 3 and 7.
