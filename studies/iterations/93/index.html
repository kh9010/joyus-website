The design system, as found

Read before drawing anything. Each line says where it comes from.

  1. White page, Space Grotesk only, no serif. Ink #2C3544, ink-soft #54606F; pink, cyan and yellow are the only accents. CLAUDE.md "Design system"; styles.css; the page went white in Study 18 and is white live (Study 40).
  2. Display type is heavy and tight: 700, tracking -0.035em to -0.04em, line height about 1. The live headline (Study 40: 700 plus a 0.9px stroke, -0.035em); work/agemo.html hero h1 (-0.04em, 0.92); Study 70's question.
  3. The accent in a headline is pink, on part of the line. Pink text is only 4.25:1 on white, so here the pink sits under the words as the live page's thick underline. work/agemo.html .accent; Study 70 "nature and"; Study 39/40 links (5px underline, 7px offset).

The four options

The six who-meets-what pairs get a treatment from her system in each, in place of 92's pink and cyan pills. Everything else (header, close, footer) is the same in all four, so the pairs and the hero are what you compare.

a · Six stones

Each kind of member is one real stone, in the stone colours, neighbours different (Study 67: one stone per thing someone has to say). The pairs sit on hairline rows under small caps column labels, like Study 13's "How it got made". Quotes at agemo's 500 weight, the close on a 2px ink rule.

b · Six cards

agemo's hypothesis cards: white, 1.5px hairline, 14px corners, a lift on hover; the stone sits where agemo put "H1". Two across on a phone, three on a desk. The quotes move into a dark ink section as agemo's voice cards, the opening quote mark in cyan.

c · A dark section

agemo's takeaways: the six pairs are the dark section, white lines on faint rules, "Who it's for" in yellow and "What fits them" in cyan, a dot in each stone colour. The quotes come back on white with agemo's pink highlighter under the phrase that matters.

d · Like the front page

The live page's grammar: the J of real stones beside the words (left of them on a desk, above them on a phone), the headline with its ink stroke, and each kind of member set as one of the front page's links, a big word on a thick pink, cyan or yellow underline, what fits them underneath in the subtitle's register.

Not sure of her intent

  • Caveat. Her June guide puts handwritten eyebrows on every case-study section; nothing since the page went white (Studies 40, 67, 70) uses it, so none of the four does.
  • Paper or white. agemo is on warm paper #FAF7F2; CLAUDE.md says the page is white now. All four are white; c and b borrow agemo's dark ink section instead of its paper bands.
  • The close. Her guide ends every case study on "Be our friends." and a "your move" label, and keeps headlines without full stops. 92 final's close and headline are Kahran's approved words, so they stay; the shape of her close (2px ink rule, big 600 line, left aligned) is used.
  • Pink as text. agemo sets accents in pink type; at 4.25:1 it misses the 4.5 rule, so here the pink is an underline or a highlighter.
  • The J and the stones are rendered once from the live worker and Gummy (stones.html, shoot.mjs --stones) and used as pictures, so the case study carries no three.js.
