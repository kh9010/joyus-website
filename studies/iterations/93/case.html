Case study: strategy

East West Club's value was big, but vague. Now it's specific.

A new members club in Seattle, not a country club. Who it was for kept moving, and so did what it offered. Fix one and the other shifts, so we solved both at once, until six kinds of member each met one clear reason to join.

Who it's forWhat fits them

  1. The joinerleagues, poker, a featured chef
  2. The newcomerevents with Seattle companies
  3. The hosta Friday night to throw, and credit
  4. The golfera simulator bay, and slower nights
  5. The second acta new start, with others starting too
  6. The regulargood nights, in and out fast

Before, no members meant no money, and no money meant nothing got built. Now founding members are signing, and the club is on schedule to open.

Two weeks with Kahran and Divya on who it's for and what it means. What it cost: $5–10K.
