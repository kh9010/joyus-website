What East West Club members actually come for.

Where would you cut this?

The café, the lounge, the events space. Wellness and the bays. Featured chefs, poker nights, golf-focused pilates — and leagues that give you somewhere to go even when you don't have friends.

Bookings he can plan around — an 8am bay before work. Demo days, whiskey launches, Bandon and Pebble. Deliberately slower programming: visits over two hours.

A named role and a real position in the club. He organizes programming, recruits teams, hosts member dinners — and we say publicly that he is the one who made it happen.

The lounge as workspace and as the default "meet me at." A room mixed on purpose — professions, genders, backgrounds. Events with the top Seattle companies, one-offs, short-format matches.

Cohorts that start the game on the same day, so nobody is behind. Leadership called out by name, and accolades worth winning. Private space to book — book clubs, birthdays, corporate parties.

Guest passes, and the generosity of bringing people. Athlete and brand nights, demo sessions, date nights worth choosing. Faster programming — in and out in sixty to ninety minutes.
