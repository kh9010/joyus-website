Strategy/EWC

Who East West Club is for, and what it is up against.

client
East | West Club, Seattle
when
March – August 2026

The competition was never another club. It was a coffeeshop, a bar, home.

24 wants: coffeeshop 4 · bar 3 · home 4 · country club 2

What's their Tuesday night like without EWC?

  1. A way to be away from his usual social circles.a coffeeshop near the house, or the office
  2. Running into someone he hasn't seen in a while.kids' sports, community events
  3. A space that reminds him of freer times.the driving range, a lakefront patio
  4. Wellness on Monday morning, before work.at home, a small private gym

We give him The café, the lounge, the events space. Wellness and the bays. Featured chefs, poker nights, golf-focused pilates — and leagues that give you somewhere to go even when you don't have friends.

  1. A simulator that's free when he wants it.his house
  2. Dropping in without the long commute.the local gym
  3. Thursday night poker.a friend's house, a private room at Daniel's
  4. Wellness — most clubs don't offer it.a scheduled appointment with his wife

We give him Bookings he can plan around — an 8am bay before work. Demo days, whiskey launches, Bandon and Pebble. Deliberately slower programming: visits over two hours.

  1. Meeting younger, more interesting people.the country club, concerts, dinners
  2. Being a practical mentor to someone.board work, informal coffees
  3. Being valued and noticed for it.charity events, winery concerts
  4. Throwing something impromptu on a Friday.the country club

We give him A named role and a real position in the club. He organizes programming, recruits teams, hosts member dinners — and we say publicly that he is the one who made it happen.

  1. A true second space.a coffeeshop
  2. Somewhere to take video calls.a coffeeshop
  3. Somewhere to meet investors.a coffeeshop
  4. People who aren't colleagues.meetups, affinity groups, friends of friends

We give him The lounge as workspace and as the default "meet me at." A room mixed on purpose — professions, genders, backgrounds. Events with the top Seattle companies, one-offs, short-format matches.

  1. Reconnecting with the hobbies they dropped.the arts centre, the mall, a purchase
  2. Friends who match the life they have now.friends of friends
  3. Seeing people without hosting at home.a favourite restaurant
  4. Being respected for what they know.charity events

We give them Cohorts that start the game on the same day, so nobody is behind. Leadership called out by name, and accolades worth winning. Private space to book — book clubs, birthdays, corporate parties.

  1. A third place.a bar
  2. Plans that are certain when she needs them to be.the neighbourhood bar
  3. People to watch the game with.a sports bar, the work text group
  4. Knowing the next thing before it's the next thing.work, Instagram

We give her Guest passes, and the generosity of bringing people. Athlete and brand nights, demo sessions, date nights worth choosing. Faster programming — in and out in sixty to ninety minutes.
