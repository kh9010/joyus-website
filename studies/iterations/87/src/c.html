Case study: East West Club, Seattle

You have the value. You can't break it down yet.

That was East West Club, a new members club: golf bays, a café, a lounge. It had a lot to offer, said the same way to everyone.

Two of us at Joyus broke it into six kinds of member: what each wants, and where they go for it today.

88%of founding members signed. Faster than they expected.
Why it mattered

Until members signed, there was no money to build. Until it was built, there were no next members.

What we found

The competition wasn't another club. It was what they'd do otherwise.

  1. The Quiet ConnectorRunning into someone he hasn't seen in a while.Today: kids' sports, community events
  2. The Country-Club GuyA simulator that's free when he wants it.Today: his house
  3. The Semi-Retired OperatorMeeting younger, more interesting people.Today: the country club, concerts, dinners
  4. The New-to-Seattle FounderSomewhere to meet investors.Today: a coffeeshop
  5. The Empty-NesterSeeing people without hosting at home.Today: a favourite restaurant
  6. The Mid-Career High EarnerPeople to watch the game with.Today: a sports bar, the work text group
In green: the club's own words, from their brand guide.
What they did with it

The six went into their brand guide, a page each.
