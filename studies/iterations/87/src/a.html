East West Club, a new members club in Seattle, had golf bays, a café and a lounge. It said all of it the same way to everyone.

At Joyus, the two of us split it into six kinds of member: what each one wants, and where they go for it now. The six went into the club's brand guide.

88%of founding members signed, faster than the club expected.

The club's founders came to us to make it tangible where the club sits in a member's life. Until members signed, there was no money to build. Until it was built, no next members.

The competition wasn't another club. It was what they'd do otherwise.

“If East West Club didn't exist, what would be missing from your life?”

If i am a golfer, i would not have the convenience. And a coach.
