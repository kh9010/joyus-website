Joyus Case study: East West Club

You have the value. You can't break it down yet.

East West Club, a new members club in Seattle (golf bays, a café, a lounge), had a lot to offer. It said all of it the same way to everyone.

Two of us at Joyus broke it into six kinds of member: what each one wants, and where they go for it today.

  • The Quiet Connector
  • The Country-Club Guy
  • The Semi-Retired Operator
  • The New-to-Seattle Founder
  • The Empty-Nester
  • The Mid-Career High Earner

Before: one pitch for everyone
