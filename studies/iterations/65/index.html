About, one thing

Study 63 kept stones and words apart. Here each page is one idea, with no panels. Open and play.

The wall open

The words are behind the stones. Move across, and they fall.

The cairn open

Every line is a stone, with the words on it. You build the pile.

The trail open

A stone leaves the letter and travels down to land beside what it says.
