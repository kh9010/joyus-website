Who East West Club is for, and what it is up against.

client
East | West Club, Seattle
when
March – August 2026

The competition was never another club. It was a coffeeshop, a bar, home.

The Quiet Connector

$400K HHI, spends on what compounds. Two kids, 9 and 12. Soccer sideline, porch coffee, house project.

  • a coffeeshop near the house, or the office
  • kids' sports, community events
  • the driving range, a lakefront patio
  • at home, a small private gym

The café, the lounge, the events space.

Wellness and the bays.

Featured chefs, poker nights, golf-focused pilates — and leagues that give you somewhere to go even when you don't have friends.

The Country-Club Guy

$600K+, asset-rich. Teens at home. 7:40 tee time, home by noon, lake with the kids. Already belongs somewhere.

  • his house
  • the local gym
  • a friend's house, a private room at Daniel's
  • a scheduled appointment with his wife

Bookings he can plan around — an 8am bay before work.

Demo days, whiskey launches, Bandon and Pebble.

Deliberately slower programming: visits over two hours.

The Semi-Retired Operator

$2M+ net worth, lives off investments and board fees. Grown kids, first grandchild. Two boards, one advisory role.

  • the country club, concerts, dinners
  • board work, informal coffees
  • charity events, winery concerts
  • the country club

A named role and a real position in the club.

He organizes programming, recruits teams, hosts member dinners — and we say publicly that he is the one who made it happen.

The New-to-Seattle Founder

$250K plus equity. Newly married, no kids, a dog. New restaurant, one hike, Sunday work.

  • a coffeeshop
  • a coffeeshop
  • a coffeeshop
  • meetups, affinity groups, friends of friends

The lounge as workspace and as the default "meet me at."

A room mixed on purpose — professions, genders, backgrounds.

Events with the top Seattle companies, one-offs, short-format matches.

The Empty-Nester

$500K HHI, house paid off, college fund done. Kids in their 20s. Long breakfast, garden, dinner with two other couples.

  • the arts centre, the mall, a purchase
  • friends of friends
  • a favourite restaurant
  • charity events

Cohorts that start the game on the same day, so nobody is behind.

Leadership called out by name, and accolades worth winning.

Private space to book — book clubs, birthdays, corporate parties.

The Mid-Career High Earner

$450K single or $700K dual, no dependents. Senior IC or top firm. Run, brunch, date night, side project.

  • a bar
  • the neighbourhood bar
  • a sports bar, the work text group
  • work, Instagram

Guest passes, and the generosity of bringing people.

Athlete and brand nights, demo sessions, date nights worth choosing.

Faster programming — in and out in sixty to ninety minutes.
