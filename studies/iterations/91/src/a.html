JoyusCase study: strategy

East West Club's value was big, but vague. We made it specific.

A new members club in Seattle, not a country club. Change who it's for, and what it means has to change too. So we worked both at once until six pairs fit.

Whoit's forWhatit means to them
  1. Quiet connectorleagues to join, no friends needed
  2. New-to-Seattle foundera lounge to work and meet in
  3. Semi-retired operatora named role, credited in public
  4. Country-club guya simulator bay before work
  5. Empty-nestera cohort that starts on day one
  6. Mid-career high earnera night out that’s quick and certain

Founding members are signing up ahead of the club's schedule.

A sign the fit is right.
