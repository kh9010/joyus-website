JoyusCase study: strategy

East West Club's value was big. We made it specific.

A new members club in Seattle, not a country club. Two questions: who is it for, and what does it mean to them? Each answer changes the other, so we worked both together until they fit.

The fit we landed on

Who it's forWhat it means to them
  1. Quiet connectorleagues to show up for
  2. Country-club guyan 8am bay before work
  3. Semi-retired operatora named role, and credit
  4. New-to-Seattle foundera lounge to work and meet in
  5. Empty-nestercohorts that start together
  6. Mid-career high earnernights out in ninety minutes

Founding members are signing up ahead of the club's schedule.

When we last spoke, the club had already reached 88% of its founding-member goal, with time to spare.

Kahran and Divya, the two of us. What it cost: $5–10K.
