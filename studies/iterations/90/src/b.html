JoyusCase study: strategy

East West Club's value was big and vague. We made it specific.

A new members club in Seattle, not a country club. Two questions: who is it for, and what does it mean to them? Change one and the other moves. So we worked both at once, until each kind of member met a reason that fit.

Where they met: six fits

Who it's forWhat fits them
  1. Quiet connectorleagues that give him a place to go
  2. Country-club guya bay he can book before work
  3. Semi-retired operatora named role that gets him the credit
  4. New-to-Seattle foundera lounge he can work and meet in
  5. Empty-nestercohorts that start on the same day
  6. Mid-career high earnernights that get her in and out fast

The fit is landing: founding members are signing up ahead of schedule.When we last spoke, the club was already 88% of the way to its December goal.
