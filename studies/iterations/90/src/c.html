East West Club's value was big and vague. We made it specific.

A new members club in Seattle, not a country club. Who is it for, and what does it mean to them? Fix one and the other shifts.

So we moved both sides until each pair fit.

Who it's for →← What it means to them
  1. Quiet connectorLeagues that give him somewhere to go
  2. Country‑club guyA bay he can book before work
  3. Semi‑retired operatorA named role he gets credit for
  4. New‑to‑Seattle founderA lounge that's his “meet me at”
  5. Empty‑nesterCohorts that start the same day
  6. Mid‑career high earnerNights she can be in and out of
Six pairs that fit: the value, made specific.

It resonates. Founding members are signing up ahead of the club's schedule.

When we last spoke, it was already at 88% of its founding-member goal for December.

Kahran and Divya, the two of us. What it cost: $5–10K.

You don't need a brief. It starts small.
