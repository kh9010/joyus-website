## Who reads it

A founder or creative, sent the link by someone they trust, who is missing one piece. They're on their phone and busy. The page has to say "that's you, and you'd come out of this looking good", not "look at us".

## The story (Kahran, 8 Nov 2026: this is the frame)

East West Club (EWC) is a new members club in Seattle, with golf bays, a café and a lounge. It is not a country club: it's a new kind of social club.

1. **The goal.** EWC needed to feel accessible and desirable to the right people.
2. **The problem.** Its value was big and amorphous: "we're a not-country-club, a new social club." That meant something to the founders and nothing in particular to anyone else.
3. **What we did.** We worked out what that value means to people. That also meant working out *who those people are*: the ones for whom it could mean something.
4. **The hard part.** It was an equation where everything moves at once. The customer base was moving (who the club is for), and so was what the club delivers. Fix one and the other shifts. The work was finding a sustainable place where they meet, a maximum or an equilibrium, rather than a guess at either side alone.
5. **What it did.** 88% of founding members signed, faster than the club expected. Before that, no money in hand meant no construction, and no construction meant no next members. The right members first is what broke that loop.

The insight is in steps 3 and 4: **who and what were solved together, as one moving equation.** Everything else on the page is evidence for it. Don't lead with any single piece of evidence (for example, the club's "Tuesday night" framing, or the member list) as if it were the idea.

## What the reader must get in the first phone screen, in plain words

1. **Who this is for:** East West Club, a new members club in Seattle. Name it in full.
2. **What the problem was:** a big, vague value that meant nothing in particular to anyone.
3. **What we did:** found who it's for and what it means to them, solved together.
4. **Ideally:** that it worked (88%).

Earlier versions failed blind readers at exactly this point. They couldn't tell:
- **who "they" or "you" was,** the club or the reader;
- **what the studio made;**
- **what the labels meant.**

So write in plain sentences. Explain no metaphor before the facts.

## How it should feel and look

- **Let pictures carry it.** Words are trying to carry too much, so the visuals should do more of the work. The moving equation is a good candidate for one strong, static, self-labelled diagram: two things that move, and where they meet. Nothing may need a tap to make sense.
- **Two voices, like our decks.**
  - Ours: commentary, with one quiet cue, such as italics.
  - The club's: their verbatim words, upright, each with a short plain source.
  - No third style. This is how we show we can do meta-analysis, and those are the clients we want.
- **Show some of the made work at a readable size:** a brand-guide page or a member card. A list of names isn't proof.
- **Show what it costs:**
  - Who did it: the two of us.
  - A price range: $5–10K, written plainly, e.g. "what it cost: $5–10K".
- **The close:** a small, safe way in that isn't a pitch: a comment, an email, or "How would you have solved it?". Use "You don't need a brief. It starts small."

## Hard rules (the repo is public)

- **Names:** no names of the club's people and no prospect names. Kahran and Divya may be named.
- **Money and figures:** no money except $5–10K, and no figure except 88%. No income or family details on member cards: wants, today-places and gives only.
- **Banned phrase:** never write "an easy weekday place".
- **Placeholders:** none visible. No "[time]", "wordmark", "TODO", internal notes, § marks or empty boxes. The wordmark is the plain text "Joyus". If you don't have something, leave it out.
- **Legibility:** body text at least 16px, labels at least 13px, and every piece of text at least 4.5:1 contrast. No em dashes in our words.
- **Length and width:** at most 4 phone screens at 390px wide. It must also be fine at desktop width.
