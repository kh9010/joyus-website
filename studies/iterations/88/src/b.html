Two of the six. The others: the Quiet Connector, the Country-Club Guy, the Semi-Retired Operator, the Empty-Nester. The competition wasn't another club. It was what they'd do otherwise.

The hard part: both sides kept moving

Change who the club is for, and what it should deliver changes. Change the offer, and different people want in. We solved both together, until they held.
