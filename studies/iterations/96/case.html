How do you leave your mark while making space for others?

Gliitch wanted to be loud.

Its work is other people's brands.

So the mark changes colour for the company it keeps.

On their picture, it is the full stop.

With no room at all, only its squares.

Seen on everything.
In nobody's way.

A brand that works for other brands needs a volume knob. Gliitch can sign every piece it makes, from a full screen down to three squares.
