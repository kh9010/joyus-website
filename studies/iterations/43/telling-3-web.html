27 May, on the FigJam board: for each persona, what they want and where they get it today. Drawn as a web, the competition shows: it is a coffeeshop, a bar, home, far more often than another club.

Each line is one want from the brand guide's "what we replace", word for word. Places are grouped by the word they share in the guide's line ("a coffeeshop", "the country club"); the grouping is ours.
