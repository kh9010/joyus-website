The record

short story · then everything

They asked for a brand. On the first call, "the anti-country club" became "just different", and the real question showed: who is this club for?

We started from real people the founders wanted in the room, sorted them into six personas, and asked what each of them uses instead today. The answer was a coffeeshop, a bar, home, far more often than another club.

Then the mix: a founding class cast by role, so the room is never one kind of person. The brand, the sales kit and the site were built on that. The club opened with its first 25 members.
