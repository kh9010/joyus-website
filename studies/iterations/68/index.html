Secret Senses, the case study

Few things: the motifs, the labels, the bottle, the family. Three ways to make them one. Scroll them. Words are placeholders.

Pull back open

One camera move: a flower, its label, its bottle, its family.

The label is the page open

Its real border is the window's edge. You read it like the label.

The motifs gather open

Loose on the table, then a wreath round the bottle.
