What the research says is wrong with the stone elements

Checked 25 November 2026.

What Study 119 does, and the rule it breaks

Study 119What the source saysSource
A button grows when reached for and its neighbours shrinkFigma tried more than 50 button prototypes for its 2024 website. A colour-blocked button whose size expanded on hover was rejected: it broke the consistent width the page needed. Time to reach a target depends on its distance and size, so a target that changes under the pointer changes the task.Figma, web system · Fitts's law
Form fields sit side by side in slanted cells that resize while typingOne column, one row per field. Label immediately above its field. Field about as wide as the expected input. Forms that follow the rules: 78% filled in right first time, against 42%.NN/g, forms
A list of clients is replaced by cells of unlike shapeGoogle's own research on expressive design names this failure: replacing a vertical list with art made usability scores fall. "No amount of expressive design will beat basic functionality." People expect a site to work like the ones they know.Google, expressive research · Jakob's law
Every piece is a full-strength colour; nothing is the main action46 studies, more than 18,000 people: a key action is found up to four times faster when it alone is set apart by size, colour and containment. Accent colour belongs on the few controls that act; content-heavy pages take a neutral theme.Google, expressive research · Google, brand in Material
Labels are centred inside irregular shapes; no two share an edgeFigma removed centred text as an option in its web system; left-aligned is the default. Body text is black or white only.Figma, web system
Everything moves on bouncing springs, every time anything is touchedTwo registers. Productive motion for button states, dropdowns and reveals: subtle, 70 to 150 ms. Expressive motion kept for occasional, important moments, 240 to 400 ms. If people keep noticing a motion, reduce it. Animation should be unobtrusive, brief and subtle.Carbon, motion · NN/g, animation
A top-to-bottom gradient and a sheen on every pieceNo source read says this in so many words. The nearest: Pentagram's Voronoi identity for Cohere uses gradients only as quiet background "atmospheres", never on a component. That each piece carries its own gloss is what reads as a 2010 button; that reading is mine.Pentagram, Cohere
Pressable targets with slanted edgesA target must hold an upright 24 by 24 px square; 44 by 44 for important controls. The thin ends of slanted pieces do not.WCAG 2.2, target size

Where brands with a shape idea put it

BrandWhere the idea livesSource
Cohere, by Pentagram, 2023. Built on Voronoi cells.The symbol: three cells at different stages of growth. The headline typeface: cell cuts spread through the letters as you type, with a variable version setting how many and where. Icons: one line each, on a 24 px square. 3D pebbles for film and stills. A layout tool that puts cells inside rectangular containers. Gradients as background. Nothing read says a button, a field or a menu takes a cell's outline.Pentagram · Creative Boom · It's Nice That
Figma, 2024. Shapes taken from its own toolbar.Shapes and motion in illustration and at Config. The website's parts were cut from 48 variants to 24, left-aligned, 29 colours of which 25 are backgrounds, text black or white.Figma, Config · Figma, web system
Dropbox, by Collins, 2017One dial. Turned up in marketing to provoke; turned down in the product, where people need to concentrate. Colour, type and illustration all move on it.It's Nice That · Collins
Instrument, on brands as systemsThe brand is carried by behaviour: timing, transitions, responsiveness, tone, density and rhythm, generated from rules.Instrument
