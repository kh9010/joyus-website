# Joyus — who this is for

_Working set of target audiences. Kahran, 2026-07-20 — for Divya to react to. Not final; a starting shape._

## The principle

The buyer is defined by their **situation**, not their **category**. Not "startup founders." The buyer is anyone at a genuine inflection who has enough at stake — and enough resource — that a premium two-person engagement pays for itself, and who wants the decisive move **and** the made thing from one pair they trust, instead of stitching a consultant to an agency.

Tying ourselves to "founders" would (1) shrink us into the most crowded, most commoditized niche there is, (2) chain the upside/equity model to cap tables specifically, and (3) miss the best buyers.

## The cast

- **Founders raising / launching / pivoting** — the obvious one. Equity is native here.
- **A person launching themselves** — an author, a creator, a public figure, someone repositioning a career or platform. The moment + the made thing applies to a *person's* launch as much as a company's.
- **An established business at an inflection** — a rebrand tied to a real strategic shift, a spinoff, a next-generation handoff of a family business, an acquisition that needs to be told as a story. These pay cash, big — no equity needed.
- **A mission / movement at its decisive moment** — a nonprofit, a campaign.

## Who it's NOT for

We take a few engagements a year, for people at a real inflection with real stakes. We are not a vendor for routine work. Naming who we turn away is what makes the selectivity legible.
