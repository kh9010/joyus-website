the workshops we run

Rooms where we figure things out together.

Half-day to multi-day rooms — for leadership teams, founder communities, and creative teams. We don't run keynotes; we run rooms that leave you with something you can use on Monday.

For communities For leadership teams Strategy & practice Half-day to multi-day

Workshops are how the thinking part of "we think with everyone, we build with a few" scales. Two arcs run through the rooms we run — a strategy arc, where founders and teams find a sentence they can say back to each other, and a practice arc, where designers, founders, and creative teams build new working muscles for AI, prompting, and visual storytelling. Different rooms, same posture.
