three ways the work page could feel.

Same problem each time: how to show that we work across many disciplines (research, brand, product, story, growth) — across many projects, each holding multiple verticals — without flattening into another agency grid.

Arrangement 02 · v2 ★ pick
tape bands

Five bands, one per discipline, each scrolling at its own speed. A project lands in every band it touches — Tatsam appears 4 times, Klydo 4. Cards are mixed: covers, type-only manifestos, dark quote cards, counters. Studio-voice interludes between bands. Per-band tinted washes. Breadth becomes a designed surface.

constellation

editorial index

A typographic table of contents. Big project names, year, and the disciplines each held listed underneath. Click any row to expand a short reframing in place. Quietest of the three — confident, restrained, lets the work breathe.
