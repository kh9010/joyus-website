Motion Jigsaw

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026.

Motion Jigsaw is a puzzle game from Joyus. This policy explains how the game handles information on Android, iPhone and iPad.

For privacy questions or requests, contact divya@joyus.studio.

The short version

You do not need an account to play, and the game never asks for your name, email address, contacts, location, camera or microphone. It sends Joyus anonymous play statistics (how puzzles are played, and in which country, not who plays them) so we can make the puzzles better. If you choose to email us feedback, we receive that email. There is no advertising, and we do not sell or share information with anyone for their own use.

Play statistics

While you play, the game records short events about the game itself and sends them to Joyus in batches when your device is online. They are:

  • Sessions: when a session starts and ends, how long it lasted, how many puzzles were started and solved, how long since the previous session and since the game was installed, the furthest puzzle reached, and whether sound and haptics were on.
  • Puzzles: which puzzle was opened and from where, its size and pattern, whether it was a replay or a resumed board, and how it ended: solved, left or closed, with the time spent, the number of moves, hints and restarts, and the sound setting.
  • Screens and settings: which screen was opened, and a setting when you change it (for example sound off).

Each event carries the game version, the platform (Android or iOS), the time, and two random identifiers made by the game itself: one for this installation and one for the current session. They are not your device's advertising ID or any hardware identifier, and they are not linked to your name, your email or any account. Reinstalling the game makes a new one.

The statistics are processed for us by PostHog, an analytics service, on its European (EU) cloud. PostHog receives your device's IP address when the game sends events, as any internet service does. It uses the address only to work out which country you are in, then discards it: we keep the country and continent, not the IP address, and not your city, region, postcode or any finer location. The country lets us see whether players in different places find the game differently.

We use play statistics only to understand how the game is played: which puzzles are too hard or too easy, where players stop, and whether a change helped. We keep them for up to 12 months. When the device is offline, events wait on the device and are sent later; the waiting file is limited in size.

Play statistics are part of how the game works and there is no switch to turn them off. You can stop them at any time by uninstalling the game.

Deleting your play statistics

To have the statistics from your installation deleted, open Settings › Delete my play statistics in the game. It opens an email to us with your installation's random identifier filled in, which is the only way we can find its statistics; send it and we delete them within 30 days. Uninstalling the game first removes the identifier from your device, so we could no longer find them.

Feedback by email

The game can open your own email app with a message to Joyus already addressed, with the game version, platform and the puzzle you were on in the subject. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you do, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and we use them to read your feedback and reply. We keep feedback emails while they help us improve the game, and delete them on request.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your puzzle progress, completed puzzles, completion times and dates, what the tutorial has shown you, and preferences such as sound and haptics on your device. This lets you resume play and keep your settings. It also writes local diagnostic logs to help investigate technical problems; these are not sent to Joyus.

The game uses vibration for optional haptic feedback, and the internet only to send play statistics.

Joyus does not operate a cloud-save service or receive a copy of your saved progress. Device or operating-system backups are separate from Joyus's services and depend on the platform, app configuration and your backup settings.

Security

Play statistics are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Game files are stored in the app's local storage and rely on your device's operating-system protections. No system can guarantee complete security.

Keeping and deleting game files

Local game files stay on your device until you remove them. Removing them deletes your saved progress and settings; Joyus cannot recover them for you.

Android: Clear the app's storage in device settings or uninstall the app. App-data backup is disabled in the current Android app configuration.

iPhone and iPad: Use Delete App to remove the app and its associated local data. Offload App preserves documents and data. Any existing device or cloud backups are managed separately through Apple's backup settings.

Children

Motion Jigsaw is made for adults and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has sent us feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of information about you, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use them, contact divya@joyus.studio. For play statistics, use Settings › Delete my play statistics so we can find them.

App stores and external links

The store or platform you use to install the game may process installation, device and diagnostic information through its own services and settings, and may make reports available to developers. This processing is covered by the platform's privacy policy: Google and Apple.

The game includes links that open the Joyus website and this policy in your browser. Visiting a website is separate from playing the game, and that site's privacy practices apply.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before the game's data practices change, including before it collects anything new. The published policy shows its latest revision date.
