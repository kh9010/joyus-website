Motion Jigsaw

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 September 2026.

Motion Jigsaw is a puzzle game from Joyus. This policy explains how the game handles information on Android, iPhone and iPad.

For privacy questions or requests, contact divya@joyus.studio.

Information stored on your device

You do not need an account to play. The current game does not include advertising, analytics tracking or an automatic reporting service that sends gameplay data to Joyus.

The game stores your puzzle progress, completed puzzles, completion times and dates, tutorial state, and preferences such as sound and haptics on your device. This lets you resume play and keep your settings. It also writes local diagnostic logs to help investigate technical problems. These files are not automatically sent to Joyus.

The game uses vibration for optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Joyus does not operate a cloud-save service or receive a copy of your saved progress. Device or operating-system backups are separate from Joyus's services and depend on the platform, app configuration and your backup settings.

Sharing and security

The game does not transmit your saved progress, settings or local logs to Joyus or third parties. We do not sell or share this locally stored information.

Game files are stored in the app's local storage and rely on your device's operating-system protections. No storage system can guarantee complete security.

Keeping and deleting information

Local game files stay on your device until you remove them. Removing them deletes your saved progress and settings; Joyus cannot recover them for you.

Android: Clear the app's storage in device settings or uninstall the app. App-data backup is disabled in the current Android app configuration.

iPhone and iPad: Use Delete App to remove the app and its associated local data. Offload App preserves documents and data. Any existing device or cloud backups are managed separately through Apple's backup settings.

We cannot access or delete game files on your device remotely. For questions about your game data, contact divya@joyus.studio.

App stores and external links

The store or platform you use to install the game may process installation, device and diagnostic information through its own services and settings, and may make reports available to developers. This processing is covered by the platform's privacy policy: Google and Apple.

The game includes a link that opens the Joyus website in your browser. Visiting that website or another external service is separate from playing the game, and that service's privacy practices apply.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change, including before introducing features that collect additional information. The published policy will show its latest revision date.
