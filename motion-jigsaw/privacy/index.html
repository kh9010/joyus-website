Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

This privacy policy describes how Joyus ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you play Motion Jigsaw (the "Game") on Android or iOS. By using the Game you agree to this policy. If you have any questions, contact us at divya@joyus.studio.

Information we collect

Information you provide. The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name or contact details. If you send us feedback from inside the Game, we receive what you write, and your email address if you choose to include one.

Information collected automatically. When you play, the Game collects usage data: which puzzles you play and how (time taken, moves, hints, restarts, whether a puzzle was solved), which screens you open, your settings (such as sound on or off), session times and lengths, the Game version and your platform (Android or iOS). This data is associated with a random identifier generated by the Game, not with your name, device or any account. We also receive an approximate location (country, region and city) derived from your device's IP address; the IP address itself is not stored.

Information stored on your device. Your puzzle progress, completion times and preferences are stored on your device so you can resume play and keep your settings. They are not sent to us.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or any hardware identifier, and the Game contains no advertising.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the Game, to understand how its puzzles are played, to fix problems, and to read and respond to your feedback. We do not use it for advertising or sell it to anyone.

How we share information

We share information with service providers who process it on our behalf and under our instructions, such as our analytics provider (PostHog, hosted in the European Union). We may also disclose information where required by law, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of our business, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

Data retention

We keep usage data and feedback for up to 12 months, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Information stored on your device stays there until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with reputable providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

We are based in India and our service providers are in the European Union. Your information may therefore be processed in countries other than your own, where it is protected by this policy and by our agreements with those providers.

Children

The Game is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at divya@joyus.studio.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always published at this address, with its revision date at the top.

Contact us

Joyus, Bengaluru, India. Email: divya@joyus.studio.

Delete my play data

To delete the usage data collected from your installation of the Game, open Settings › Delete my play data in the Game. It prepares an email to us containing your installation's random identifier, which we need to find your data; send it and we will delete the data within 30 days. You can also email us directly, but without the identifier we may not be able to locate it.
